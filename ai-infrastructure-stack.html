The AI Infrastructure Stack

From your prompt down to the electrical grid: who owns each layer, and how the money actually flows. The two things worth remembering — everyone rents from someone, and power is the constraint that gates the whole thing.

Seven layers, top to bottom

Read it like a section drawing. You sit at the top with a prompt; each layer below is provided by someone else, until you reach the physical foundation.

Your app / agent
prompt in → tokens out · the agent loop (Sessions 5–6)
Where you build. Cost = tokens in + tokens out × price; agents multiply the call count.

BUY — hosted API  ↔  BUILD — self-host on rented GPUs

The expensive, fast-depreciating asset — and the collateral behind neocloud loans.

Equinix · Digital Realty · QTS · Vantage — owned by PE/infra (e.g. Blackstone)

01Power
Power & grid — the binding constraint
utilities · power-ready land · grid connections · cooling
Scarce power, not concrete, is what gates the whole stack. Whoever controls secured power holds the leverage.

How the money & iron actually flow

The surprising part isn’t the value chain — it’s the loops. The copper arrows are the non-obvious flows: NVIDIA funds the buyers of its own chips, and the hyperscalers rent GPUs from the neoclouds.

Two dials underneath the stack

The same infrastructure gets packaged along two axes that decide most enterprise AI deals.

Sovereignty is a spectrum

1 · In-region public cloud Normal cloud kept in-territory with contractual/technical controls (Microsoft Cloud for Sovereignty).
2 · Partner-operated “trusted” cloud A local company runs it: Bleu (FR, Capgemini+Orange), Delos (DE, SAP), S3NS (FR, Thales).
3 · Separate / air-gapped cloud AWS European Sovereign Cloud (EU-only staff, runs cut off); Google Distributed Cloud (air-gapped).

Ask: who operates it? where do data + keys live? can it run cut off? More sovereign = fewer latest features + higher cost.

Buy vs. build (= cloud vs. on-prem)

Buy

Hosted API. Fast, lean, no infra. Access to frontier closed models you couldn’t build.

Pay per token; costs scale with usage.

Build

Self-host open weights on rented GPUs. Control, privacy, edge, cost-at-scale.

You own the serving stack, ops & uptime.

Realistically “build” = rented dedicated GPUs in someone else’s building — not your own data center.
